LUCKY WORLD

Windows: right-click the zip, choose "Extract All...", pick a normal folder
  (Desktop or Documents; not OneDrive, and don't run it from inside the zip),
  then double-click LuckyWorld.exe in the extracted folder.
  (The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the
   game isn't signed: click "More info", then "Run anyway".)
Linux: unzip, then double-click LuckyWorld (or run ./LuckyWorld).

The game reads its "data" and "assets" folders beside it, and also has a
copy of every one of those files built in: if Windows or antivirus blocks a
file ("Access is denied"), it uses its own copy and lists the file in
lucky-world-notes.txt. If it still can't start, it writes why in
lucky-world-error.txt beside it.

How to play
  The main menu: New Game (choose a world or a random one, the difficulty
  and the game's length), Continue (your newest save), Tutorial (a short
  guided game: start here), Load, Settings, Help, Quit.
  You spin the wheels for your nation, then rule it on a 3D world map
  among up to 24 rivals who spun the same wheels.
  Win by conquest (hold half the map), economic power (2.5 times the next
  economy for 10 years), diplomacy (lead an alliance holding 60% of the
  map for 5 years) or the best score when the game's length runs out. Lose
  if you're conquered, overthrown in a civil war or made a vassal (a vassal
  can play on and fight for independence).
  Hover any number or button for what it is (and its shortcut key).

The mouse does everything
  Wheels:   click Spin, or the wheel itself; Skip spin rolls the rest.
  Map:      left-drag moves it, the mouse wheel zooms, right-drag turns
            and tilts it; double-click a nation to centre on it;
            right-click a nation for its country card, diplomacy, a treaty,
            trade, spies or war.
  Windows:  the buttons on the right (Economy, War room, Battles,
            Diplomacy, Spies, News log); tabs, rows, sliders and buttons
            inside; the X or a click outside closes them.
  Menu:     the Menu button (top right): save, load, settings, help,
            main menu, quit.

Esc never throws you out
  Esc closes what is open; with nothing open it opens the pause menu.
  Leaving to the main menu or the desktop always asks first and saves
  your game.

Shortcut keys (all optional)
  Wheels:       Space spin · S skip spin
  Map:          WASD pan · Q/E turn · R/F zoom · T/G tilt · H home
  Clock:        Space pause · 1-5 speed
  Windows:      O economy · K war room · J biggest battle · P diplomacy
                I spies · L news log
  Saving:       F6 quick save · F9 quick load (the game also saves every year)
  Full screen:  Alt+Enter (full screen is the default)
  Settings can change every map key, the picture quality, full screen, the
  window size, the music and effects volumes and the size of the menus.
  Saves are in the "saves" folder beside the game; settings in
  lucky_world.settings.toml.
