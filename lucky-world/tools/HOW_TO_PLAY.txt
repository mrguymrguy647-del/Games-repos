LUCKY WORLD

Windows: right-click the zip, choose "Extract All...", pick a normal folder
  (Desktop or Documents; not OneDrive, and don't run it from inside the zip),
  then double-click LuckyWorld.exe in the extracted folder.
  (The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the
   game isn't signed: click "More info", then "Run anyway".)
Linux: unzip, then double-click LuckyWorld (or run ./LuckyWorld).

The game reads its "data" and "assets" folders beside it, and also has a
copy of every one of those files built in: if Windows or antivirus blocks a
file ("Access is denied"), it uses its own copy and lists the file in
lucky-world-notes.txt. If it still can't start, it writes why in
lucky-world-error.txt beside it.

How to play
  The main menu: New Game (choose a world or a random one, the difficulty
  and the game's length), Continue (your newest save), Tutorial (a short
  guided game: start here), Load, Settings, Help, Quit.
  You spin 16 wheels for your nation, then rule it on a 3D world map among
  up to 24 rivals who spun the same wheels.
  Win by conquest (hold half the map), economic power (2.5 times the next
  economy for 10 years), diplomacy (lead an alliance holding 60% of the
  map for 5 years) or the best score when the game's length runs out. Lose if you're
  conquered, overthrown in a civil war or made a vassal (a vassal can play
  on and fight for independence).
  Hover any number for what it is and what changes it. F2: help.

Keys
  Menus:        Up/Down choose · Enter go · Esc back
  New Game:     1-3 a world · 4 or R random world · D difficulty · L length
                Space spin · S skip spin · W watch NPC brains
  World map:    drag or WASD pan · right-drag or Q/E turn · wheel or R/F zoom
                click a province · C country card · V map overlays · M terrain
  Clock:        Space pause · 1-5 speed
  Economy:      O      War room: K      Biggest battle: J
  Diplomacy:    P      Spies: I         Notifications: L
  Saving:       Esc the pause menu (save, load, settings, help, menu)
                F6 quick save · F9 quick load · the game saves itself every year
  Help:         F2
  Settings can change every map key, the picture quality, fullscreen, the
  window size, the music and effects volumes and the size of the menus.
  Saves are in the "saves" folder beside the game; settings in
  lucky_world.settings.toml.
